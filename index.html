Set up Padala Ledger

A few quick steps to configure your GCash agent POS: starting balances, commission tiers, ATM fee presets, and a PIN to protect the app.

Step 1 of 4

Starting balances

Enter what you're actually holding right now — physical cash, and your GCash wallet balance.

Step 2 of 4

Commission tiers

Fully editable. "Per unit" scales by chunks (e.g. ₱30 per ₱1,000); "Flat" charges one fee for the whole bracket. Edit, add, or remove rows anytime in Settings.

Step 3 of 4

ATM withdrawal fee presets

Save the fees different ATMs charge per withdrawal (e.g. Landbank via GCash card), so logging a withdrawal is one tap.

Step 4 of 4

Create your PIN

This handles cash records, so we'll lock the app behind a 4–6 digit PIN.
